Privacy Choices

MiniAppsLab does not require a cloud account, so most core data can be managed directly on your iPhone.

Last updated: August 27, 2026

1. Delete local projects and versions

You can delete projects or versions you no longer need inside MiniAppsLab. To remove local app data more broadly, use the relevant iOS app storage or delete-app controls available on your device.

Back up first: if a project exists only on your device, ELLEZ generally has no server copy to restore after deletion.

2. Manage device permissions

Photo, file, or other iOS permissions can be adjusted through the options iOS makes available for MiniAppsLab in Settings. Some features may stop working when the related permission is disabled.

3. Apple analytics and diagnostics

Whether Apple shares analytics or crash information with developers is controlled by the Apple privacy and analytics settings on your device. Those are iOS/Apple settings, not MiniAppsLab account settings.

4. Access or delete support information you sent to ELLEZ

If you previously sent support emails, attachments, or other personal information to ELLEZ, email ellez.tech.2023@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. We may need to reasonably verify that the request comes from the relevant email account or data subject.

5. Third-party data used by imported mini apps

If code you import connects to a third-party API, website, analytics tool, or login service, use that provider's privacy controls and policies to manage the information it received. ELLEZ cannot delete data already sent to a third party on your behalf.

6. Need help?

Visit MiniAppsLab Support or email ellez.tech.2023@gmail.com.
