Privacy Policy

MiniAppsLab is designed to be local-first. Core project files and version history are intended to remain on your device, and a MiniAppsLab account is not required.

Effective: August 27, 2026 · Last updated: August 27, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the MiniAppsLab iOS application provided by ELLEZ LLC, the official MiniAppsLab web pages, and information you voluntarily provide when contacting us for product support.

Key point: HTML, CSS, JavaScript projects and version data you import are designed to be stored locally on your device. MiniAppsLab does not need to upload those projects to ELLEZ servers to provide its core functionality unless you intentionally share them, send them to us for support, or the code you imported independently connects to an external service.

2. Information we process

Local projects and settings

MiniAppsLab may store project files, versions, project names, icons, preferences, and other information needed to operate the app within storage made available to the app on your device.

Information you provide to support

If you contact us by email or another support channel, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, problem description, device or OS version, screenshots, attachments, or diagnostic information. We use it to respond to support, security, bug, or privacy requests.

App Store and diagnostic information

App Store downloads, payments, refunds, and certain diagnostics are processed by Apple. If you allow Apple to share analytics or crash information with developers, we may receive diagnostic or aggregated information through Apple developer services. MiniAppsLab does not directly receive your full payment card details.

3. Device permissions and user actions

  • Files/Documents: when you choose to import a project, the iOS document picker lets you select content to make available to MiniAppsLab.
  • Photos: features such as saving a Home Screen icon may request the permission needed to save an image when you initiate that action.
  • Clipboard: content you explicitly paste can be used to create or update the project you selected.
  • Shortcuts: Home Screen launching may use Apple Shortcuts or system-provided App Intent/URL flows.

Installing MiniAppsLab does not automatically grant the app access to all files, photos, or other data on your device.

4. Imported mini apps and third-party network activity

MiniAppsLab can run HTML, CSS, and JavaScript that you import. If your code references external websites, fonts, APIs, images, analytics tools, login services, or other network resources, that mini app may connect directly to those third parties.

Those connections are determined by the code you imported and are not the same as ELLEZ collecting that information. Third-party handling is governed by the applicable third party's policies and your configuration. Do not run code from a source you do not trust.

5. Advertising, tracking, and analytics

The current MiniAppsLab product design does not depend on third-party advertising networks and is not intended to build cross-app or cross-site advertising profiles. If a future version adds analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs that materially change data handling, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before or with that release.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local project data generally remains until you delete the project, clear related app data, or remove the app.
  • Because core project data is not stored in an ELLEZ account or cloud workspace, we generally cannot remotely read, recover, or delete the projects stored only on your device.
  • Support emails and attachments may be retained as reasonably necessary to resolve the request and meet legitimate legal or security needs, then deleted or de-identified as appropriate.

See Privacy Choices for management options.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No device, email system, or network transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Back up important projects and avoid importing untrusted code.

8. Children and sensitive information

MiniAppsLab does not rely on user accounts or advertising profiles as a core feature. We discourage putting sensitive personal information into untrusted mini apps. If you build or import tools for children or other protected groups, you are responsible for ensuring the code, external services, and data practices comply with applicable law and platform rules.

9. Your privacy choices and rights

For information actually held by ELLEZ, such as support correspondence, you may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Email ellez.tech.2023@gmail.com with the subject “MiniAppsLab Privacy Request.”

For projects that exist only on your device, delete them in the app. If the app has already been removed, ELLEZ cannot remotely delete or restore that local-only data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect product changes, legal requirements, or platform requirements. Material changes may be communicated through the updated date on this page, in-app notice, or another reasonable method.
