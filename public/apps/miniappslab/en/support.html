3. Frequently asked questions

Are my projects uploaded to ELLEZ?

MiniAppsLab is designed to be local-first. Imported projects and versions are intended to stay on your device. If the HTML or JavaScript you import connects to an external API or website, that connection is determined by your code and the third party.

Can ELLEZ recover a project after I delete the app?

If the project existed only on your device and you did not create another backup, ELLEZ generally has no server copy to restore. Keep an export or other backup of important projects.

Why doesn't tapping the Shortcut add it to the Home Screen?

During setup, open Apple Shortcuts and press and hold the relevant MiniAppsLab project shortcut, then choose “Add to Home Screen.” A normal tap usually just runs the shortcut.

Who handles refunds?

Payments and refund requests for App Store purchases are managed by Apple through its purchase history and refund processes.
