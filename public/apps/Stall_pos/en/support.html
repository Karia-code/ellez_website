FAQ

Q: Is Stall POS completely offline?

A: Yes, all data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload any data, ensuring your business privacy.

Q: Can I sync data across multiple devices?

A: Cloud sync is currently not supported. This is to ensure data privacy and offline stability, allowing you to use it reliably at markets without internet access.

Q: Is the app free?

A: It is free to download and try. We offer "Monthly Subscription" and "One-time Purchase" options for you to choose from.

How is Stall POS Lite different from the full Stall POS?

Lite focuses on the shortest booth checkout flow: create products, arrange frequent items, checkout, review the current sales session, and manually close it. The full Stall POS provides broader product, inventory, reporting, and advanced operational features.

Does Lite automatically close the sales session at midnight?

No. Lite does not split sales by calendar day. Until you tap “Close Session,” the same session continues even across midnight or after reopening the app.

How much is Lite?

Stall POS Lite has a regular one-time price of US$9.99, with the release date still to be announced. The full Stall POS is also available as Gold (one-time) or Yen (subscription).
