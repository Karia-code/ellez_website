Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Stall POS, offline use, privacy, and supported workflows.

Basics

Does Stall POS require an internet connection?
No. Stall POS is designed with an offline-first architecture. Checkout, product management, and local statistics can continue without a network connection.
Do I need an account?
No account is required. You can open the app and start using it without creating a cloud login.
Where is my sales data stored?
Sales and product data are stored locally on your device. Stall POS is designed to avoid uploading your business records to ELLEZ servers.

Features & Fit

Is Stall POS intended for restaurants or cafés?
Not primarily. Stall POS is designed for retail, creator markets, pop-ups, and exhibitions. It does not currently focus on table management, kitchen display systems, or complex menu modifiers.
Does it connect directly to card terminals or e-invoice hardware?
Stall POS focuses on lightweight checkout and record keeping. You can record the payment method after using a separate payment terminal, but direct hardware integrations are not the core workflow.
Can I export sales reports?
Yes. Sales data can be exported as CSV for spreadsheet, accounting, or inventory follow-up.

Compatibility & Data

How is Stall POS different from a cloud POS?
The main differences are lightweight operation and privacy. Many cloud POS systems depend on accounts, connectivity, and recurring services; Stall POS is built around a simpler local workflow for individual sellers and short-term booths.
Is Android supported?
Stall POS currently focuses on iOS / iPadOS.
What happens if I delete the app?
Because business data is stored locally, deleting the app can remove that local data. Export important records regularly and keep device backups where appropriate.
Is Stall POS free?
Stall POS provides a 7-day free trial. Long-term access to the complete product is unlocked through the purchase options shown in the App Store.

Stall POS Lite

How is Stall POS Lite different from the full Stall POS?
Lite focuses on the shortest booth checkout flow: create products, arrange frequent items, checkout, review the current sales session, and manually close it. The full Stall POS provides broader product, inventory, reporting, and advanced operational features.
Does Lite automatically close the sales session at midnight?
No. Lite does not split sales by calendar day. Until you tap “Close Session,” the same session continues even across midnight or after reopening the app.
How much is Lite?
Stall POS Lite has a regular one-time price of US$9.99. The release date has not been announced; the final price shown in your regional App Store may vary.
