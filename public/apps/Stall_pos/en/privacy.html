Stall POS Family Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Stall POS and Stall POS Lite provided by ELLEZ LLC. Both apps are built around local data processing and offline use. If a specific edition later adds network-based features, the relevant data handling details will be updated on this page.

Data Collection and Use

Stall POS is a completely offline POS system. We value your privacy and explain our data handling practices:

  • All data is stored only on your device.
  • We do not collect or upload any transaction data.
  • No account registration is required.
  • We do not track your usage behavior.

Stall POS Lite Data Handling

  • Lite does not require a user account.
  • Products, cart data, the active sales session, and closed-session summaries are stored locally on the device.
  • Lite does not actively upload sales records to ELLEZ LLC servers.
  • The app accesses a product image only when the user explicitly selects one.
  • Exports such as CSV are initiated by the user; storage and sharing of exported files are then controlled by the user.

Data Security

Your data security is paramount to us:

  • Data is stored only in your device's local storage.
  • It is not uploaded to the cloud or other servers.
  • We recommend backing up important data regularly.

App Permissions

Stall POS requires the following permissions to function properly:

  • Storage: Used to store transaction records and product data.
  • Bluetooth: Used to connect to Bluetooth printers (optional feature).
